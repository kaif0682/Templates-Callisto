The condition

Gynecomastia is a benign enlargement of glandular tissue in one or both sides of the male chest. It is not usually harmful, but it can cause tenderness and often affects confidence.

Causes

Usually a shift in the balance of hormones. Common triggers:

  • Puberty
  • Adult hormonal change
  • Medications
  • Anabolic steroids and supplements
  • Weight change
  • Underlying medical conditions

Some causes need a medical check first.

Gland or fat?

True gynecomastia involves glandular tissue. Pseudogynecomastia is fat alone. Many chests are a mix, and the difference decides the treatment. Only an examination can tell.

Severity

Doctors often grade severity from I to IV. Only an in-person exam can confirm yours.
